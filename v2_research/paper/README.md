# GLASSCHIP-V2 paper package

**Status: manuscript complete and submission-ready.** All prose drafted, all citations

## Positioning

An **HPC/systems empirical measurement-quality study** — how measurement quality affects
thermal-model identification on a supercomputer, and whether better measurements make the
residual more predictable. **Not** a PINN paper, a new model, a monitor, a digital twin, or a
degradation/failure study.

## Terminology (used consistently throughout)

"measurement quality" (not telemetry fidelity); "temperature and power measurements" (not
telemetry); "effective thermal response time (effective τ)", always an identification
parameter, never a physical R·C constant; **"116 sampled host-sockets"**, never "the fleet" —
Summit has 4,626 nodes and the 116 units are a sample of it; **F0 is the reference measurement
regime**, not physical ground truth.
