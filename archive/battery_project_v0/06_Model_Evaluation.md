# 18. Evaluation Metrics

## RMSE

Measures prediction error.

Lower is better.

---

## MAE

Measures average prediction error.

Lower is better.

---

## R² Score

Measures goodness of fit.

Interpretation:

- R² = 1.0 → Perfect
- R² > 0.95 → Excellent
- R² > 0.90 → Good

Higher is better.

---

Additional Metrics:

- Physics Residual Error
- Training Loss
- Validation Loss
